A. Narrative report

1. Brief summary

MESCL has been in operation for one year. In that period we registered a tremendous achievement in the sale of ISC products and appliances — Cookits, solar box cookers, parabolic cookers and energy-saving stoves (small, medium and large) — in various villages in Mayuge and nearby districts in the Eastern Region.

So far the total sales of our appliances have been good and promising, although they were low in the beginning months and during the months of heavy rain, i.e. March and April.

Through our coordinator Mr. Alewijn we managed to secure financial support from SCK KoZon and commenced our MESCL ISC business. The support was received as follows:

  • UGX 13,240,800 received 03/05/2022
  • UGX 5,130,000 received 11/11/2022

Our main source of funding has come from SCK KoZon. Allow me to extend my sincere appreciation to my coordinator Mr. Alewijn Van Asperen and the entire SCK board for the great work you are doing in empowering different communities to save the environment as they cook safely.

Your technical support and contributions — guidance, financial support, and technical knowledge in report writing and data presentation on Excel sheets — have really helped us all the way in the growth of our young ISC business at MESCL.

Mr. Alewijn recommended us to attend training on linking and learning in Kampala and a visit to Mr. Didacus Odhiambo. Both opportunities were very helpful to the business — in community development and participation, and in being inspired by Mr. Didacus respectively. Not forgetting the networking part of it.

b. To what extent have these activities been carried out?

To a large extent the planned activities were done, though we did not hit our monthly target as projected in the budget proposal, due to the high fuel prices in the country at the beginning of the project.

Secondly, community awareness about our Integrated Solar Cooking appliances (ISC) was low, being a new product in the region. Gradually the sales picked up in the subsequent months.

2.2 Outcome

What was the objective of the project?

The objective of MESCL is to embrace solar energy appliances in Mayuge and nearby districts while saving the environment.

To what extent is this objective reached?

To a large extent our objective has been covered, as people have now embraced solar energy and are conserving the environment. The sales of our ISC products have been generally good.

Who is benefiting directly from the project, and in which way?

The manager, shop keeper, and the ambassadors/volunteers benefit directly, since we earn a salary and commission respectively from the project and are able to support our families directly (as attached in the Excel spreadsheet of beneficiaries).

The landlord of the premises we rent, together with our customers and the entire community, benefit indirectly — they are able to use safer methods of cooking while saving the environment.

3. Lessons learned

What did you learn from the implementation process of this project?

  • 1 Through the various demonstrations, home visits and group cooking parties, I realised that integrated solar cooking appliances have a big untapped market — on such days, 80% of the items you go with are bought and taken.
  • 2 Unexpected situations such as pickup hire for our commodities need to be planned for in future, as it was expensive transporting the appliances at the time of demonstrations and home/group cooking parties.
  • 3 Through the linking and learning meeting in Kampala I learnt that networking is paramount in project development and implementation for a sustainable project.
  • 4 Through clean energy cooking, the health of women, the elderly and the children who are involved in cooking is in most cases improved.

4. Next phase

  • • We hope to encourage customers to purchase ISC products by paying in instalments while the items are still in the shop, taking the appliance after completing payment in full.
  • • My first priority in the next phase is to expand the project with briquette making, as demand for them is high — people come to the shop asking for them from time to time — and later to expand with conical cookers.
  • • More demonstrations and home/group cooking parties need to be improved, as most of the time we sell most of the items on such occasions. This is a way of improving our marketing techniques.
  • • Regarding transport arrangements, we will continue to hire a pickup to transport our products to and from demonstration centres, as we ask you to support us in securing affordable means of transport to reduce the high transport costs.
  • • I was invited to the Moroto region (Napak) to share knowledge and skills about our ISC appliances. The people there requested that we look at having a branch for them in Moroto, as it is very far from Mayuge to purchase these appliances — something to think about in future.
